# J.E. Rocker GovTech Client Onboarding Protocol

**Namespace**: Business 1 &bull; GovTech Contracts & Procurement Intelligence  
**Scope**: Post-Payment Execution & Client Onboarding Sequence  

---

## 1. Welcome Statement
Welcome to J.E. Rocker GovTech Advisory. Your transaction has cleared our payment gateway and your account is initialized in our primary procurement tracking cluster (`data/govtech_clients.json`).

---

## 2. Onboarding Timeline (First 72 Hours)
- **Hour 0–2**: Client record validated; automatic ingestion trigger dispatched to SAM.gov & state scraper indexing.
- **Hour 24**: Execution of the entity registration verification sweep (UEI, CAGE, and SBA classification checks).
- **Hour 48**: Capability Statement gap analysis and target NAICS clustering run completed.
- **Hour 72**: Official **GovTech Readiness Report** compiled and delivered to client inbox with prioritized bidding actions.

---

## 3. Required Documentation Checklist
Clients must supply the following core assets:
1. **Capability Statement**: Current 1–2 page PDF or Word document outlining past performance and core equipment/services.
2. **Current SAM.gov Snapshot**: Proof of active status, expiration date, or current registration confirmation PDF.
3. **Primary Point of Contact**: Designated executive authorized to commit company proposals.
4. **Target Past Performance Summaries**: Details on top 3 commercial, state, or federal projects executed within the last 36 months.

---

## 4. Next Steps Queue
1. **Intake Review**: Local operations team verifies target states (AZ, CA, TX, FL, VA, NY) and NAICS profile against active solicitations.
2. **Scraper Filter Setup**: Client keywords configured in local matcher database.
3. **Client Kickoff Call**: 30-minute alignment session to review primary target vehicles and agency procurement schedules.
